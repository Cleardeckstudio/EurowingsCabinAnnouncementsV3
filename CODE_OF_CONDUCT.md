# Code of Conduct

This project welcomes feedback, bug reports, and suggestions from the flight simulation community.

- Be respectful and constructive in Issues and comments.
- Personal attacks, harassment, or sharing other people's private information will not be tolerated and such content will be removed.
- Off-topic or abusive content may be closed without response.

We aim to respond to Issues in a neutral, factual, and courteous manner, in the language they were raised in when possible.
